audio-sfx.css, audio-sfx-samples-1.css, audio-cries-1.css to audio-cries-5.css, audio-music-1.css and
audio-music-2.css hold, after the build, Generation III
sound data from the pret/pokeemerald decompilation, https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald (commit 9103e52): sound effect
and music sequences converted with its mid2agb tool, voicegroup entries, key split tables, programmable wave and

only. None of this data is in the repository. Build it with source/audio/download.sh and source/audio/build-audio.mjs.
