audio-sfx.css and audio-cries-1.css to audio-cries-5.css hold Generation III sound data from the pret/pokeemerald
decompilation, https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald (commit 9103e52): sound effect sequences converted with its
mid2agb tool, voicegroup entries, m4a driver tables, and the Pokemon cries compressed with its wav2agb tool. The sound
contents are copyright Nintendo, Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK Inc. They are included for personal use only. Rebuild
with source/audio/download.sh and source/audio/build-audio.mjs.
