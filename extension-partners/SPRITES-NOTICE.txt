partners-icons.css, partners-fronts.css, partners-anim.css and partners-ui.css bundle Generation III graphics from the pret/pokeemerald
decompilation, https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald: party icons, front pics with their shiny palettes, trainer
overworld sprites, the second front animation frames, the summary screen, naming screen, type icon and battle healthbox graphics, and the small font.
partners-dex.css holds the normal and small fonts and move and ability descriptions from the same repository. A few fronts fall back to the PokeAPI
sprites repository, https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites (CC0 1.0). All image and text contents are copyright Nintendo,
Creatures Inc. and GAME FREAK Inc. They are included for personal use only. Stats, learnsets and the evolution table
come from the MIT-licensed pokemon-showdown package. Rebuild with source/partners/build-assets.mjs and
source/partners/build-ui.mjs.
